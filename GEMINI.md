# Suno Songwriter Extension

This extension provides two primary skills for AI music creation:

1.  **suno-songwriter**: Specialized in formatting and enriching lyrics with technical Suno AI tags ([Verse], (ad-libs), etc.). Use when the user has raw lyrics or needs to finalize a song prompt.
2.  **album-concept-designer**: A creative director for building complex conceptual albums. It handles world-building, storyline, tracklists, and narrative track specs. 

### Workflow Integration
The `album-concept-designer` handles the creative conceptualization and raw lyric generation. It then hands off the technical formatting to the `suno-songwriter` skill.
